Contribute your home language

If you are a student in the department, you can document your own language and add it to the repository. CoRSAL provides training and guidance, so no prior archiving experience is needed.

  1. Get trained. Attend a CoRSAL session on recording, consent and metadata.
  2. Record and describe. Collect natural speech and document it with a speaker, place and context.
  3. Submit for review. Send your materials to the department. Reviewed work is deposited in the archive.
